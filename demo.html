Watch the model, then question it.

This is a compressed recording of Project 1's actual browser viewer. The mint G1 skeleton is generated motion. The warm ghost is a target constraint. Style selections and movement commands alter the planner's inputs; the target slider and overlay are inspection tools.

H.264, 1600 × 724, 30 fps, about 6.7 MB. The audio track was omitted because the interface demonstration requires no sound. Open the MP4 directly.

Look for three separate signals

  • Command: which style or movement button is active?
  • Target: how does the warm ghost's pose or path change?
  • Result: how does the mint skeleton follow or depart from that target?

When a change feels delayed, inspect the panel's Status and underrun readout before attributing it to the model.

What the clip cannot prove

A short recording cannot establish sustained 50 Hz streaming, physics stability, GPU speed, or Unreal readiness. The live demo on this laptop used CPU inference and a kinematic G1 skeleton. The control field guide turns this clip into six experiments; the build journey gives the measured limits.
